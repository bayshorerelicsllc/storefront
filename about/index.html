About us

Railroad slide collectors turned sellers — putting thousands of rescued 35mm slides back into the hands of people who love them.

Our story

Bay Shore Relics LLC is a small family business in Green Bay, Wisconsin. We started as railroad slide collectors ourselves — the kind who light up at a box of old 35mm slides at an estate sale. Over the years our collection grew to roughly 40,000 slides and counting, far more than any one collection could ever display. So we decided to sell: every duplicate, every overflow box, every slide that deserves a better home gets listed here, one at a time.

What we sell

We sell vintage 35mm railroad slides: steam and diesel locomotives, freight and passenger consists, depots, yards, and mainline scenes, photographed over the decades by railfans and railroaders across America. Each listing is a single physical slide — when it sells, it's gone for good.

Second-hand honesty

We are a second-hand store, and we're upfront about that. These slides come from collections we bought over the years, and their full history is unknown to us. We do not guarantee that any slide is an original — some may be duplicates or reproductions made by others, and some mounts or labels may carry markings we can't explain. We never make reproductions ourselves, and we describe each slide as honestly as we can from what we see. What we promise is this: the listing photo shows the actual slide you'll receive, and what you see is what you get.

The single-item promise

Every listing represents one physical item. There is no stock, no backorders, no "more on the way." When you buy a slide, we remove the listing immediately and no one else can buy it. If a listing is on the site, the slide is here in Green Bay, ready to ship.

Questions about a slide, an order, or a return? Get in touch — we reply within 1–2 business days.
