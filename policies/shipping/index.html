Shipping

Packed with care in Green Bay, Wisconsin, and shipped via USPS.

Where we ship from

Every order ships from Green Bay, Wisconsin (ZIP 54311) via USPS.

Handling time

We pack and ship within 1–2 business days of your order. Slides travel in protective packaging so they arrive the way they left.

Shipping rates

Rates are calculated at checkout from your destination — enter your address and you'll see the exact cost before you pay. Buying several slides? Shipping is charged once per order, not per slide.

Wisconsin sales tax

Orders shipping to a Wisconsin address are charged Wisconsin sales tax at checkout. Orders shipping anywhere else in the U.S. are not charged sales tax by us.

International orders

We ship internationally via USPS. Please note: duties and taxes are not included at checkout, cannot be calculated in advance, and are the buyer's responsibility — your country's customs office or the delivering carrier will collect them on delivery. Delivery times vary by country.

Tracking

Every shipment includes tracking. Once your order ships, you can follow it from your order tracking page.

Questions or problems

If a package arrives damaged or goes missing, contact us right away and we'll make it right. Changed your mind instead? See our returns policy.
