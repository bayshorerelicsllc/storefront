Accessibility

Everyone should be able to browse and buy slides here. We're working toward that, one fix at a time.

Our commitment

Bay Shore Relics LLC aims to make this shop usable for as many people as possible. We follow widely accepted accessibility practices (WCAG 2.1, level AA, as our target) and we keep improving as we hear from visitors.

Built-in accessibility toolbar

On every page you'll find a row of buttons in the bottom-right corner. They work instantly and your choices are remembered on your device:

  • Larger text — increases text size across the site.
  • High contrast — boosts contrast for easier reading.
  • Reduce motion — turns off animations and movement.
  • Dark mode — switches the shop to a dark theme.

What else we've done

  • Keyboard navigation works throughout the shop — you can tab through menus, products, and checkout.
  • Images carry text descriptions where it matters.
  • Color is never the only way we communicate information (sale prices, stock status, and errors all use text too).

Known limitations

Checkout is handled by PayPal and Stripe on their own secure pages, so their accessibility depends on their interfaces. If you run into a barrier anywhere on our site — a page that doesn't read well, a button that's hard to use — tell us and we'll fix it.

Feedback

Accessibility feedback goes straight to the top of our list. Contact us and choose "Other" as the subject, or just describe the problem — we reply within 1–2 business days.
